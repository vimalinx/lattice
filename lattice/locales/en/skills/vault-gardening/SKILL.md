# When to use
- “Tidy up my vault”, “My notes are a mess”, “Are there any duplicate cards?”.
- Regular maintenance (say, once a month).

# Steps
1. **Checkup** (read-only):
   - list each kind of content, and look at the relationships with graph;
   - Orphaned: notes and cards with no links at all that don't belong to any topic;
   - Duplicates: cards or notes with similar titles whose content mostly overlaps (check with search and read);
   - Missing links: the body mentions the title of an existing card or note but has no [[link]] to it;
   - Outdated: plans and progress files long past due and not updated;
   - Belonging nowhere: content that clearly belongs to a topic but hasn't been attached to it.
2. **Tidy-up plan** ⏸ Checkpoint: by category, list the problems found and the suggested handling (which two to merge, which links to add to which note, which items to attach to which topic, which ones to suggest the learner delete), each with its basis. **Show the learner first; don't change anything directly.**
3. **Make the changes** (only the parts the learner agreed to):
   - Add links: change the body with edit, or add a relation with link;
   - Merge duplicates: merge the content into the one being kept (edit), and ask the learner to delete the other one themselves (deleting belongs to the learner alone);
   - Belonging: change the properties with link or edit.
   Make each kind of change separately, so it's easy to undo.
4. **Report**: in your reply, say in a sentence or two what you did and what is left for the learner to decide.

# Don't
- Don't delete anything; deleting is done by the learner alone.
- Don't change “My understanding” (the `## 我的理解` section) or the stage.
- Don't rewrite the learner's notes on a large scale without their agreement.
